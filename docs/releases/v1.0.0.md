## bitwise-bus 1.0.0: inferring bus protocols from captures, measured on synthetic ones

From a capture file to a Kaitai Struct definition: frames, checksum, message types and typed fields, each claim with its evidence in bits. **Everything here has been measured on synthetic captures with a known answer and on the published test vectors of the CRCs. No real capture has gone through it**, and an answer on one is a hypothesis with its evidence, to be checked against what the device is known to do.

### What it does

- **`bitwise analyze CAPTURE`** cuts a capture into frames at the long gaps, at flags with byte stuffing (SLIP, HDLC/PPP), from a sync byte and the length a frame says it has (UBX, MAVLink), or as lines of text (NMEA 0183); finds the checksum (66 CRCs of 8 to 64 bits, five sums, written in bytes or as hexadecimal text, a CRC over a byte that is in no frame, with tolerance for a line that is not perfect); puts the frames into message types (frames of several sizes of one kind, kinds of lines of text); and splits them into typed fields (constants, enumerations, counters of several bytes, floats, lengths, text up to a separator, payload).
- **`bitwise export CAPTURE`** writes a message type as a Kaitai Struct definition. The real Kaitai compiler accepts it and the parser it makes reads every frame back into the same bytes, checked in CI.
- **`bitwise synth`** makes captures of six protocols with a known answer (Modbus RTU, UBX, MAVLink 1 and 2, NMEA 0183, SLIP, PPP in HDLC-like framing), and of protocols **made up from a seed** (`random`, `random-text`), with noise (a byte that arrives wrong, a frame that does not arrive). The same seed gives the same capture on every platform. **`bitwise protocols`**, **`crc-find`**, **`crc-catalog`**, **`capture-info`** and **`--version`** complete the commands.
- **`bw_core`**, the library (static, C++17, headers readable as C++11): capture, model, evaluation, generators, every stage, `analyze` and the exporter.

### API

From 1.0 the API follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): `find_package(bitwise-bus 1 CONFIG REQUIRED)` accepts any 1.x. [docs/api.md](https://github.com/juanlumc1988/bitwise-bus/blob/main/docs/api.md) says which headers are installed, which are bound with [bridgefex](https://github.com/juanlumc1988/bridgefex), what is frozen in 1.x (names, signatures, struct members, enumeration values, option defaults, the commands, the capture format, what the Kaitai exporter writes for a model, the named protocols' captures) and what is not (what the pipeline infers, the words that `describe_analysis` and `bitwise` print, the protocols made up from a seed, the ABI).

### Results

On synthetic captures, with the thresholds that the tests pin ([roadmap](https://github.com/juanlumc1988/bitwise-bus/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md#thresholds)):

- **1500 protocols made up from a seed** (framing by gaps, by a sync byte and a length, or by flags; one to four kinds of message; fields of every kind the pipeline claims; a checksum or none), 200 frames each: every frame found exactly, the checksum found and right in 99.8% of the types where the frames can show one, none wrong, none found where there is none, 90% of the true field boundaries found. With one frame in fifty with a byte wrong: 99.5% of the frames, the checksum in 97.8%; with one in twenty wrong and one in twenty lost: 97.5% and 91.2%, still none wrong but 2 in 3710.
- **1000 protocols of text** (a comma, a semicolon, a bar or a space; one to three kinds of sentence; a checksum or none): the kinds of sentence found in 1966 of 1966 cases, the checksum right in 99.4%, as many fields as the truth's in 99.5%; 96.5% and 97.5% with one frame in twenty wrong and one in twenty lost.
- **The six named protocols**: NMEA 0183 comes out as its three kinds of sentence with 18, 16 and 13 fields, as many as the truth's; MAVLink 2 as its four messages, those that come in several sizes with a length and the payload that it sizes; UBX, MAVLink 1, PPP and SLIP as their types, with the Fletcher-8 of UBX, the CRC with the hidden byte of MAVLink and the FCS of PPP found.
- Random bytes come out as one payload field and no checksum; a capture with no structure that a stage can use says so.

It is tested with gcc and clang (`-Werror`), AddressSanitizer and UBSan, on Linux x86-64, aarch64 and armv7 (qemu), macOS and Windows (MSVC x64 and arm64, MinGW).

### What 1.0 leaves out

Real captures; numbers that are only small (a byte with zeros next to it) and numbers in text (a latitude is text); the length of a kind that has one size; fields smaller than a byte; terminators other than the line feed, and stuffing other than RFC 1662 and SLIP's; two layouts of a checksum that explain the same frames are two answers, and none is reported; exporters other than Kaitai (Wireshark Lua, DBC, a C++ header); live capture. The [roadmap](https://github.com/juanlumc1988/bitwise-bus/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md) says what was left out, and why.

### Install

Archives for Linux (x86-64, aarch64, armv7), macOS (universal) and Windows (x64 and arm64 MSVC, x64 MinGW) are attached, with `SHA256SUMS`. `bitwise --version` prints `bitwise 1.0.0`.
